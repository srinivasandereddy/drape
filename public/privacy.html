Drape privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Drape is a personal wardrobe and outfit app. It is built so that your data stays with you: there is no Drape server, no advertising and no analytics. This page explains exactly what the app handles and where it goes.

What Drape stores

  • Clothes you add: photos, type, colors, fabric and other details you enter, and shop links you paste.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: name, age, gender, height, weight, city, daily routine, style choices, jewellery metal, dosha quiz answers and app colors. All optional except what you choose to enter.
  • Outfits you mark as worn, your "Love it" / "Don't like" feedback, and trips you plan.

Where it is stored

  • On your phone, in the browser's storage for this app, separately for each Google account that signs in.
  • In your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only Drape can read (Google's "app data" folder). This keeps your closet in step across your phones and works as a backup. It does not appear in your normal Drive files, and Drape cannot see any of your other Drive files.

Nobody else, including the maker of Drape, can see your closet or profile.

Google sign-in

Drape asks Google for these permissions only:

  • Your name, email address and profile photo (openid, email, profile): to know whose closet to open and to keep different people's closets apart.
  • Drape's own app data folder in your Drive (drive.appdata): to save and sync your closet. This permission does not allow access to any other file in your Drive.

Your Google sign-in token stays on your phone and is only sent to Google.

Services Drape talks to

  • Google (sign-in and Drive), as described above.
  • Open-Meteo (weather). Only the map position of the city you chose, or of a place you type, is sent, to get the forecast. No name, email, photo or closet data is sent.

Shop links you paste are read on your phone; Drape does not visit the shop or share anything with it. Background removal, color reading, outfit suggestions and the assistant all run on your phone.

How Google user data is used

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide Drape's features to you: signing you in and saving your own closet to your own Drive. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not used to train any AI model. Drape's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • In Drape: Settings → Delete my whole closet removes your pieces, photos, outfits, feedback and trips from your phones and your Drive.
  • Settings → Remove my account from this phone removes it from that phone only.
  • In Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Drape → Delete hidden app data, then Disconnect from Drive.
  • You can also remove Drape's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Drape is not directed at children under 13. Google's own account rules apply to anyone who signs in.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes too. For questions, write to the support email shown on Drape's Google sign-in screen.
